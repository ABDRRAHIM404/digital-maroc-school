STIL DYALK — LOCAL WEBSITE

1. Extract the whole ZIP into one folder.
2. Open index.html in a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser.
3. Keep assets/, images/, products/, and style.css in their original locations.

All images, fonts and icons are included. No internet, installation, server,
JavaScript, or account is required to view the website.

The shopping bag uses HTML/CSS controls. It does not persist a multi-product
cart between pages, submit orders, or process payments.

Edit text and prices in the HTML files. Edit the appearance in style.css.
Product images are in images/. Third-party fonts/icons are in assets/.
